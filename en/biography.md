# My Biography

*From my roots in the countryside to Information Technology.*

> **I am not ashamed of where I came from. I am proud of the journey I have made.**

This is an evolving account of the experiences that shaped my life. Not everything was easy, but every stage helped make me who I am today.

## Chapter 1 — My roots: childhood in the countryside

My story began long before Information Technology. As a child, I worked in agriculture and learned the meaning of hard work and responsibility at an early age.

I helped care for pigs and chickens and worked with crops including corn, sugarcane, tomatoes, and other produce. Physical work was part of everyday life.

My family faced financial difficulties. At times, I had to balance demanding work with my studies. Finding enough time and energy was not easy, but I understood that education was an important part of building a different future.

**I feel no shame about any of those jobs. On the contrary, I am proud of them.** They taught me perseverance and the value of every opportunity.

## Chapter 2 — Why I chose technology

Choosing Information Technology did not mean rejecting my roots. I saw technology as a way to improve my financial circumstances and offer a better future to my family.

I wanted to grow professionally, learn new skills, and pursue opportunities that had once seemed out of reach.

Today, I work with infrastructure, networks, servers, support, and information security. I am a **Second Lieutenant and temporary officer in the Brazilian Air Force**, working in IT at the Brazilian Air Force Logistics Institute (ILA).

My early jobs, financial challenges, and determination to study remain part of who I am.

**Technology is a new chapter in my life, not a rejection of where I came from.**

## Chapters to come

I plan to expand this biography with more memories and experiences:

- **Education and opportunities:** my journey into IT
